# SCOPE

## Surround-view Collision Prediction via Panoramic Oriented TTC Estimation

**Accepted for publication in IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO).**

Chunyu Feng, Yeqiang Qian<sup>†</sup>, Changlin Li, and Ming Yang<sup>†</sup>

School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University  
Key Laboratory of System Control and Information Processing, Ministry of Education

<sup>†</sup> Corresponding authors: [Yeqiang Qian](mailto:qianyeqiang@sjtu.edu.cn) and [Ming Yang](mailto:mingyang@sjtu.edu.cn).

## Overview

SCOPE is a panoramic collision prediction framework that estimates **Oriented Time-to-Collision (OTTC)** from surround-view cameras. It combines collision urgency with relative motion direction to assess risks across a **360° field of view**. A unified range-view representation aggregates multi-camera features into a continuous domain. We evaluate SCOPE on densified nuScenes-SceneFlow and Waymo-SceneFlow benchmarks, with additional zero-shot evaluation on a real vehicle platform.

### Highlights

- **Surround-view perception:** A unified range-view representation brings multi-camera features together for panoramic collision risk assessment.
- **Direction-aware prediction:** OTTC combines TTC with relative motion orientation to distinguish collision threats from safe pass-by motion.
- **Dense supervision and evaluation:** Densified scene flow provides pixel-level supervision, with evaluations on two benchmarks and a real vehicle platform.
